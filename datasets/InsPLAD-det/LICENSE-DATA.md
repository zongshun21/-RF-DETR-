# InsPLAD data license and attribution

The files in `annotations/` and derived files in `audit/` originate from the
InsPLAD object-detection dataset. They are redistributed under the dataset's
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

Source: <https://github.com/andreluizbvs/InsPLAD>

Paper: A. L. B. V. e Silva et al., “InsPLAD: A Dataset and Benchmark for Power
Line Asset Inspection in UAV Images,” *International Journal of Remote
Sensing*, vol. 44, no. 23, pp. 7294–7320, 2023.
<https://doi.org/10.1080/01431161.2023.2283900>

The image files are not included here. Obtain them from the official InsPLAD
distribution and comply with its non-commercial and attribution requirements.
